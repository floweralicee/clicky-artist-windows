What is Clicky?
---------------
Clicky is a little blue helper that sits next to your mouse cursor.
You hold a button on your keyboard, ask a question about your screen,
and Clicky talks back — and can point at buttons and tools for you.

It works with Maya, After Effects, Blender, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve,
Toon Boom Harmony, and other apps you use every day.


BEFORE YOU START
----------------
You need:
  • A Windows 10 or Windows 11 computer (64-bit)
  • A working microphone (built-in laptop mic is fine)
  • An internet connection (for sign-up and AI answers)


STEP 1 — DOWNLOAD AND UNZIP
----------------------------
1. Go to the Clicky website:
     https://www.floweralice.me/clicky

2. Enter your email address.

3. Click "download windows".
   Your browser will save:  clicky-windows-artists.zip

   Direct link (if you already entered your email):
     https://www.floweralice.me/clicky/download/clicky-windows-artists.zip

4. Find the zip file in your Downloads folder.

5. Right-click the zip file → "Extract All…" (or "解压全部").

6. Open the folder called  Clicky  (created when you unzip).

7. Inside the Clicky folder you should see:
     Clicky.exe          ← this is the app
     HOW-TO-USE.txt      ← this file
     HOW-TO-USE-中文.txt ← Chinese version of this guide
     (and other support files — that is normal)


STEP 2 — FIRST TIME YOU OPEN CLICKY
------------------------------------
1. Double-click Clicky.exe.

2. If Windows shows a blue "Windows protected your PC" warning:
     • Click "More info"
     • Click "Run anyway"
   (This is normal — the app is not from the Microsoft Store.)

3. If Windows asks to use your microphone:
     • Click "Yes" or "Allow"
   Clicky needs your mic so you can talk to it.

4. A sign-up window will appear.
     • Click "Get started"
     • Enter your email and a password
     • Click "Create account"

   Already have an account? Click "Already have an account? Sign in"


STEP 3 — CLICKY IS RUNNING
---------------------------
After you sign in:

  • A small blue dot appears near your mouse cursor.
  • A blue circle icon appears in the bottom-right of your screen
    (the system tray — click the ^ arrow if you don't see it).

Clicky runs in the background. There is no big main window.
That is normal.


STEP 4 — ASK CLICKY A QUESTION
-------------------------------
This is the most important part!

1. Open any app you want help with (Maya, After Effects, etc.).

2. Hold these three keys at the same time:
     Ctrl  +  Alt  +  Space
   (Hold all three. Don't tap them one at a time.)

3. While holding those keys, speak your question clearly.
   For example:
     "Where is the render settings button?"
     "How do I add a keyframe here?"
     "What does this panel do?"

4. Let go of the keys when you are done talking.

5. Wait a few seconds. Clicky will:
     • Read your screen
     • Think about your question
     • Speak the answer out loud
     • Move the blue dot to point at things on screen (when helpful)


STEP 5 — OTHER WAYS TO TALK TO CLICKY
--------------------------------------
  • Say "Clicky" out loud (wake word) — then ask your question.
    (You can turn this on or off from the tray menu.)

  • Press Esc to stop Clicky if it is still talking.

  • Say "next" or "continue" if Clicky gave you steps and you want
    the next step.

  • Say "repeat" to hear the last answer again.


STEP 6 — THE SMALL PANEL AND TRAY MENU
---------------------------------------
Right-click the blue circle icon in the system tray (bottom-right).

From the menu you can:
  • Show Panel / Hide Panel — a small window with Clicky's status
  • See how many free sessions you have left
  • Sign out
  • Quit — closes Clicky completely

The panel shows things like:
  "Listening…"  — Clicky heard you
  "Thinking…"   — Clicky is working on your question
  "Speaking…"   — Clicky is answering out loud


FREE SESSIONS AND SUBSCRIPTION
-------------------------------
  • You get 10 free sessions when you sign up.
  • Each time you ask Clicky a question, that uses one session.
  • When your free sessions run out, Clicky will show a screen
    offering a subscription for $10 per month.
  • Click "Subscribe — $10/month" to pay in your web browser.
  • After you pay, Clicky unlocks unlimited sessions automatically.


TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS
---------------------
  • Speak clearly and keep questions short.
  • Make sure the thing you are asking about is visible on screen.
  • Use headphones if Clicky picks up its own voice from your speakers.
  • Keep Clicky running — don't close it between questions.
  • If the blue dot disappears, check the tray icon is still there.


TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------

Problem: I don't see the tray icon
  → Click the ^ (up arrow) next to the clock in the bottom-right.
  → Look for a small blue circle.

Problem: Clicky doesn't hear me
  → Windows Settings → Privacy → Microphone
  → Turn ON "Let desktop apps access your microphone"
  → Make sure your mic is not muted in Windows sound settings.

Problem: "Failed to load Python DLL" or app won't open
  → Install this free Microsoft update, then try again:
     https://aka.ms/vs/17/release/vc_redist.x64.exe
  → If that doesn't help, download a fresh copy from:
     https://www.floweralice.me/clicky/download/clicky-windows-artists.zip

Problem: Sign-in or sign-up fails
  → Check your internet connection.
  → Try again in a minute.
  → Make sure your password is at least 6 characters.

Problem: Clicky says I've used all my free sessions
  → Subscribe for $10/month from the screen Clicky shows you,
    or sign in again if you already subscribed.

Problem: Windows SmartScreen blocks the app
  → Click "More info" → "Run anyway"
